Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Pre-release draft. The owner’s support contact and final public domain will be added before this URL is submitted to an app store.

What Qybeq stores

Qybeq stores game progress, settings, earned stars, unlocked visual themes and daily challenge results on your device. The web version uses browser local storage.

Analytics

The mobile apps may use Yandex AppMetrica and the website may use Yandex Metrica to understand installs, sessions and anonymous gameplay events. Analytics is enabled only after consent. Qybeq does not send names, contact details, puzzle-board contents or exact pointer coordinates.

Advertising

Optional rewarded advertising may be provided by the Yandex Advertising Network. A player chooses whether to watch an ad in exchange for a gameplay benefit. If an ad is unavailable, Qybeq may provide the hint without an ad.

Your choices

You can disable analytics, music, sound effects and notifications in the application settings. Clearing browser data removes locally stored web progress.

Service providers

Read the Yandex Privacy Policy for information about Yandex services.
