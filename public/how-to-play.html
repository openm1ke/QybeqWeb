How to play

  1. Six coordinate dice mark the cells that cannot be covered.
  2. Drag all eight pieces onto the 6×6 board.
  3. Rotate or flip a selected piece when its shape allows it.
  4. Cover every free cell without overlaps to solve the puzzle.

Finish within one minute for three stars. A slower solution earns two. A solution completed with help earns one.
