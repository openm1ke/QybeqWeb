How to Play

The board

Every puzzle starts with six dice — one for each row, A to F. Each die blocks one cell in its row. Blocked cells stay empty.

Your pieces

Eight pieces, thirty squares — exactly enough to cover every cell the dice leave free.

Rotate

Tap a piece in the tray to turn it a quarter turn.

Flip

Some pieces can also be mirrored with Flip — it acts on the piece you touched last. It is optional: every puzzle can be solved by rotating alone.

Place

Drag a piece onto the board and it snaps to the grid. The outline shows where it will land; red means it would cover a blocked cell or another piece. Tap a placed piece to take it back.

Fill the board

Place all eight pieces so every free cell is covered — no gaps. Any arrangement that fills the board solves the puzzle.
